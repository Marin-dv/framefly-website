Sep 28, 2026Naming

It's called Framefly now

The working name had to go. Framefly is what the product does: an agent that moves through your app like a fly on the wall, and turns what it sees into frames. The key-light lime the interface was already built around turned out to be the color of a firefly.

The new mark is a viewfinder locked on a subject, with its flight path drawn as onion-skinned frames. Same day: a launch date, October 28, and this site.

Sep 25, 2026Capture

Why Framefly doesn't screen-record

The same 20-second scenario was captured five ways. Every real-time method stuttered: 20 to 34 unique frames per second, with freezes of up to 0.9 seconds, because the browser renders and encodes on the same CPU the page needs. Real-time capture also silently dropped 2x density back to 1x.

Deterministic capture fixed it: virtual clocks and one rendered frame per 1/60 s of page time gave 55 unique frames per second, no freeze longer than one frame, and true 3200x1800 frames. The first attempt came out with a blank main area, because CSS animations froze with the clock. The fix steps every running animation forward on each frame.

Sep 25, 2026Agent

The agent's first real exploration

Pointed at the Framefly interface itself, the agent mapped six screens, ranked them by importance, and wrote a six-section storyboard of about 46 seconds with voice-over lines and more than 40 replayable steps. It took 5 minutes 39 seconds.

Along the way it found two of its own broken selectors by replaying its plan, and fixed them. It refused every destructive or financial button and listed each one. Its weak spot: it walked back and forth between screens instead of one continuous path, which wastes camera time. That's the next thing to fix in the planner.

Sep 25, 2026Voice

Timing comes from real speech

Storyboards first assumed narration at 150 words a minute. Real synthesized speech ran at about 190. So section lengths now come from the generated audio, never from an estimate, and the voice is generated before a single frame is filmed.

The voice leaderboards also moved this year, so the provider will be picked by ear: the same six lines through four candidates, labels hidden.
