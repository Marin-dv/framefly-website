Questions

Something missing? Write to support@framefly.app.

What kind of apps does it work with?

Web apps at launch: anything that runs in a browser behind a login, including apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Replit or v0. Chrome extensions and Framer plugins are in beta. Figma, VS Code and Webflow come later.

Do I have to give it my real account?

No, and you shouldn't. Framefly asks for a dedicated demo account. It can use the account as it is, fill it with realistic sample data if you allow it, or call a reset hook before every shoot so each take starts from the same state.

My app has 2FA, SSO or runs on localhost.

For authenticator codes, paste the 2FA secret and Framefly generates the codes. For email codes and magic links, sign the demo account up with a Framefly inbox. For Google or Microsoft SSO, import a session in one click with the Helper extension. For localhost or a staging server, run npx framefly tunnel 3000.

A free preflight checks all of this before any credit is used. Framefly never tries to get around a captcha or a bot wall. It publishes fixed IPs and a signed header you can allow instead.

What happens if a video fails?

Your credit is held when the render starts and only charged at delivery. If anything fails along the way, the credit is released in full. If you're not happy with a delivered video, you get one complete re-shoot for free within 7 days.

Can I edit the video afterwards?

Every video includes 10 targeted revisions. Ask for a change on one section ("hold on the total for two more seconds") and only that section is filmed again. Voice, music, captions and brand changes only need a new edit. Pro and Studio also export an editable project for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro.

Is the voice AI? Will people know?

Yes, the studio voices and presenters are generated. Every export carries C2PA content credentials, and the end card has one short line saying so, as the EU AI Act requires. You can also record your own narration, section by section, and Framefly aligns the captions to it.

When can I use it?

Framefly launches on Wednesday, October 28. Beta testers get in before that. Leave your email above and you'll get one message on launch day.
