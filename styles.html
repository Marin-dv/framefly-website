Twelve styles. Mix any three.

A style is a spec the renderer follows: pacing, transitions, type, palette, captions, music and camera. Every preview here plays that spec on a made-up app.

No style in this category yet.

The mixer.

Pick a main style and a second one, then choose which style owns each facet. A facet always comes from exactly one style, so two styles can never fight over the same decision.

In the app you can add a third reference and hand it any facet, the same way.

Bring your brand kit.

Any style can take your logo, colors and fonts. Brand kits check every color pair for contrast, then apply to captions, titles and the end card.
