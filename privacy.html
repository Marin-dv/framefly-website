Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. It counts its own page views as totals per day, without knowing who you are. The only personal data it collects is what you type into the launch list or the beta form, and it's used for that and nothing else.

Who is responsible

Marin de Vanssay, sole trader, operating framefly.app, is the controller of the data described here. For any privacy request, write to support@framefly.app.

This policy covers the Framefly website before launch. The Framefly product has its own privacy policy, published here before launch day, covering accounts, connected apps and videos.

What we collect

The launch list

When you press "Notify me", we store your email address and the date you signed up.

The beta application

When you apply for the beta, we store your email address, your app's address, the kind of product it is, the videos you need, when you plan to ship, whether you have a demo account, and, if you fill them in, your name and your note.

What we don't collect

  • No cookies and no local storage for tracking.
  • No analytics from another company, no advertising pixels, no session recording.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts or videos. Fonts, code and films are served from this site.

If you switch between the light and the dark theme, your browser remembers that choice on your device. It is never sent to us.

Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps technical logs of requests, including IP addresses, to run and protect its service. We don't receive or use those logs.

Our own server, which receives the forms, keeps the same kind of technical log for a short time and counts requests per address in memory to stop abuse. Your address is not stored with what you send.

Counting visits

We count how many times each page is opened and which site a visit came from (a search engine, for example), as totals per day. The numbers tell us whether the site is read. They say nothing about you.

To count a visitor once a day, our server works out a one-way code from your address and your browser. The code is replaced every day at midnight, so it cannot be used to recognise you the next day, and your address itself is never stored. No cookie is set and nothing is kept on your device for this. Basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

Why, and on what basis

  • Launch email: to send you one email when Framefly launches. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Beta application: to review your application, reply to you and, if your app is picked, set up the beta with you. Basis: steps taken at your request before an agreement (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

We never sell your data or use it for advertising, and we never add you to a newsletter.

How long we keep it

  • Launch list: until 30 days after the launch email is sent, then deleted, unless you create a Framefly account.
  • Beta applications: until 6 months after the beta ends, then deleted.

You can ask us to delete your data earlier at any time.

Who else handles it

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States) hosts this website. Transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Form submissions are stored on a server we run ourselves, in France. No other company stores them.
  • The service that sends our emails, acting on our instructions only. It is named here before any email is sent.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy, and withdraw your consent. Write to support@framefly.app and we'll answer within one month. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A change that affects data you already gave us will be emailed to you first.
