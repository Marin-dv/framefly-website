Questions

Something missing? Write to support@framefly.app.

What kind of apps does it work with?{{icon:plus}}

Web apps at launch: anything that runs in a browser, behind a login or not, including apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Replit or v0. Chrome extensions and Framer plugins are in beta. Figma, VS Code and Webflow come later.

Do I have to give it my real account?{{icon:plus}}

No, and you shouldn't. Framefly asks for a dedicated demo account. It can use the account as it is, fill it with realistic sample data if you allow it, or call a reset hook before every shoot so each take starts from the same state.

My app has 2FA, SSO or runs on localhost.{{icon:plus}}

For authenticator codes, paste the 2FA secret and Framefly generates the codes. For email codes and magic links, sign the demo account up with a Framefly inbox. For Google or Microsoft sign-in, SMS codes and passkeys, import a session from your own browser. For localhost or a staging server, run npx framefly tunnel 3000.

A free preflight checks all of this before any credit is used. Framefly never tries to get around a captcha or a bot wall. It uses fixed addresses and a signed header you can allow instead.

What happens if a video fails?{{icon:plus}}

Your credit is held when you generate and only charged at delivery. If anything fails along the way, or you stop it, the credit comes back in full. If you are not happy with a delivered video, you get one complete re-shoot for free within 7 days.

Can I change the video afterwards?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Point at the picture and say what should change, or open the plan again and edit it. Only the sections concerned are filmed again. A new voice, new captions, other music or another look only need a new edit. Ten revisions come with every video, and Pro and Studio also export an editable project for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro.

Is the voice AI? Will people know?{{icon:plus}}

Yes, the studio voices and the presenters are generated. Every export carries content credentials, the end card says so in one line, and a label shows whenever a presenter is on screen, as the EU AI Act asks. You can also record the narration yourself, section by section.

Framefly launches on Wednesday, October 28. Beta testers get in before that. Leave your email below and you get one message on launch day.
