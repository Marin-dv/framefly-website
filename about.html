Made by one developer, in public.

Framefly is built by the person who answers your email. No team to hand you over to, and every number shared along the way.

MV
Marin de VanssayMaker of Framefly and Vanssay, France

I build small products for people who ship websites and apps. Under the Vanssay name, that's plugins used on live Framer sites: reviews, an Instagram feed, access control, geo-routing and maintenance pages. I build them alone, while studying engineering.

Every launch needs a video, and a demo video is a skill of its own: recording, retakes, a steady cursor, editing, a voice, captions, every format. Plenty of good products launch without one, or with a shaky screen recording, because the person who built the product doesn't have a free week to become an editor.

Framefly is that week, done by an agent that understands your app. You stay the director: nothing is filmed before you approve the storyboard. I hold it to one test before calling it finished. Its own launch video has to be made by Framefly.

Questions, ideas or a product you'd like filmed: support@framefly.app, or follow the build on X.
