Your app stays yours.

Letting an AI click around your product is a real decision. Here is what Framefly can see, what it can never do, and what it keeps.

The AI never sees a password.

Secrets are encrypted in a vault and only decrypted inside the short-lived worker that films your app. The model writes a placeholder, the browser fills in the real value. Signing in is never filmed.

Encrypted at rest

AES-256-GCM, one data key per workspace, the key itself held by AWS KMS in Frankfurt.

A placeholder for the model

fill("#password", {{secret.password}}) is all the model ever writes.

Deleted when idle

Secrets of an app with no video for 90 days are purged. Ephemeral mode deletes them after every video.

An audit log

Who decrypted what, when, and for which video.

Three checks on every action.

Before the browser runs a click, a form fill or a navigation, the action passes three layers. If the agent is blocked more than five times in one exploration, it stops and tells you.

A hard block list

Inside the browser: payment domains are cut off at the network, DELETE requests are refused unless you allow them, and clicks on destructive words are stopped in ten languages.

A guard model

A second, faster model reads each proposed action with the page around it. Anything risky is blocked unless you allowed it.

Your own rules

Allow or deny by URL, CSS selector or button text, for one app or for all of them.

Always blocked, even if you allow it: Paying for anythingDeleting the accountChanging the login email, password or 2FAMessages to anyone outside the Framefly inboxGranting access to a third partyGetting around a captcha

If your app sits behind Cloudflare or a firewall, Framefly does not try to sneak past it. It uses fixed outgoing addresses and signs every request with an X-Framefly-Agent header, so you can allow it in one rule.

What is kept, and for how long.

Framefly is hosted in the EU. For the data visible in your demo accounts, Framefly is your processor under the GDPR and a data processing agreement is available. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Labeled as AI, the way the law asks.

Since August 2, 2026, article 50 of the EU AI Act requires AI-generated media to be marked. Framefly does it by default, so your video is compliant without you thinking about it.

  • Content credentials (C2PA) and XMP metadata in every exported file
  • One line on the end card, "Voice generated with AI", styled to match your brand
  • A corner label whenever a presenter is on screen. It cannot be switched off.
  • Consent first: cloning a voice or a face starts with you reading a random phrase aloud, and only works for yourself
